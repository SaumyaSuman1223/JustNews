# JustNews audit: 2026-09-29 (third pass)

**Method:**
- Two independent reviewers: a design review of the live pages and the source, and the Impeccable detector (source scan plus in-page injection).
- My own technical checks.

**Scope:**
- A production build against the local API, after a fresh ingest (925 new articles) and a new Aquila edition.
- 20 routes, in light and dark mode.
- Widths: 1440, 390 and 320.
- Checks:
  - axe (WCAG 2.2 AA)
  - tap targets
  - overflow
  - weight
  - LCP and CLS
  - failed requests
  - screenshots

**Local-data caveats:**
- The local encoder barely clusters stories, so three real multi-language stories were grouped by hand for this review and removed afterwards.
- There are no reader clicks, so Most read stays hidden.
- No source has a Perspectives role, so the topic view's Perspectives stay hidden.
- Signed-in flows were not tested.

## Fixed and holding

- **Read in** works signed out and shrinks to "EN" on a phone.
- **Across languages** quotes each language's headline and counts outlets.
- **Today's Aquila** teaser.
- Weather, US markets and companies are opt-in, and markets are honestly labelled.
- **Phones:**
  - They open on the lead and its row before the rail.
  - Three even tabs.
- **Discover:**
  - Every card shows its age.
  - Six topics first, then "N more".
  - Share-this-feed and the duplicate sliders button are gone.
  - Cards use the editorial radius.
- **Pages and prompts:**
  - One sign-in gate pattern.
  - The signed-out heart explains itself.
  - Display applies choices immediately.
  - The 404 has a title and search.
  - The admin gate has a heading.
- **Detail:**
  - Buttons use the page font.
  - Images are right-sized from publisher CDNs, and failed images become text cards.
  - Fonts are self-hosted.
  - The dark mode still holds.

## Priority issues

1. **[P0] The consent choice can't be made on a phone.**
   - The banner sits under the tab bar (z-index 20 against 40). Tapping Decline or Accept hits the tab bar; checked with `elementFromPoint`.
   - The copy is a double negative: "Nothing you save or mark isn't affected either way."
   - Fix: raise the banner above the tab bar, or offset it by the tab bar's height. Rewrite the line.
2. **[P1] The story page doesn't let you compare outlets.**
   - Every card's source line says "7 sources" and links back to the same page.
   - The outlet list links to `/a/{id}`, while card headlines open the publisher: three link targets for one article.
   - The language chips do nothing. The same "7 sources" loop appears in the clustered article page's "Also reported by" cards.
   - Fix:
     - On the story page, name each card's outlet (favicon and name).
     - Make the chips jump links to each language's section.
     - Consider compact rows (outlet · headline · time) grouped per language, instead of image tiles repeating the same photo.
3. **[P1] Top turns into one publisher's firehose after card 12.**
   - `stream()` in `lib/discover.ts` puts 12 importance-ordered stories first, then pure chronology. 18 of the first 30 on `/en?view=top` are The Hindu.
   - Aquila's selection has the same weakness.
   - Fix: cap each source's share across the whole page, and boost multi-source, multi-language clusters in Top and in Aquila's lead.
4. **[P1] Story metadata is in the wrong language.**
   - `/hi/story/20` shows an English h1 with no `lang`, and an English "Politics" (the category label skips `curatedTopicLabel`).
   - `/en` says "Science and technology" where the rest of the site says "Tech & Science".
   - Fix: take the title and dek from the reader's language where the story has a report in it, tag `lang`, and localise the category.
5. **[P2] Language names in sentences are endonyms.**
   - "also in हिन्दी" and "también en English".
   - Fix: use `Intl.DisplayNames` for names inside sentences, and keep endonyms for pickers.
6. **[P2] Devanagari typography.**
   - Letter-spacing on tracked labels breaks Devanagari shaping (Hindi Aquila).
   - Latin figures inside Hindi headlines fall back to Cormorant's old-style figures.
   - Hindi headlines look much heavier than Latin ones in a mixed feed.
   - Fix: `:lang(hi)` resets the tracking and uses lining figures; match display weights across scripts.
7. **[P2] Phone chrome and search.**
   - The top bar, Discover bar and tab bar take 22% of an 844px screen.
   - On phone search, four filters push the first result to about 700px.
   - Fix: hide the top bar when scrolling down, and put the filters behind "Filters (n)".
8. **[P2] NDTV images all fail.**
   - Its CDN returns 403, which Chrome blocks (`ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ORB`).
   - They fall back to text cards, but much of Hindi Discover loses its pictures.
   - Fix: check a source's image host at ingest, and store no image for a host that refuses. Or test from a normal browser first; the block may be aimed at data-centre traffic.

## Smaller findings

- **Phone drawer:** it shows the desktop "Collapse sidebar" button. An unconditional rule later in `globals.css` overrides the media-query `display: none`.
- **Tap targets under 24px:**
  - Story header source links (23px) and category link (15px).
  - Search's "Clear" (17px).
  - Source names in the older list cards (19px).
  - On desktop, `.sources` (19px) and "Pick topics" (22px).
- **Image sizes:**
  - Search thumbnails declare a 20rem width but show at 128px.
  - Cards on full-width pages (source, story) declare 16rem but render around 360px.
- **Stale or inaccurate copy:**
  - `aquila.backHome` still says "Back to the front page".
  - `search.intro` promises "summaries"; the product shows snippets.
  - `signIn.settings.body` implies reading languages need an account.
  - The Privacy page's public draft banner stays.
  - A comment in `lib/navigation.ts` mentions rail subtitles that no longer exist.
- **Consistency:**
  - Narrow centred pages (Settings, Display, Login) sit beside full-width left-aligned ones (Saved, Following, Search).
  - Aquila's reader nav offers Saved and Following to signed-out readers.
  - The article page stacks a brass rule and a black rule 18px apart.
  - A tracked-caps "Filed under" label remains.
  - `/admin` shows its full navigation to signed-out visitors.
- **Templated leftovers:**
  - The sign-in card repeats "Sign in" as heading and button, with the button styled as secondary.
  - The login page is a stock auth card with an "OR" divider.
  - The empty search state uses the same centred card.
  - Coverage chips look like buttons but do nothing.
  - Discover rows stretch cards to equal height, leaving gaps under short headlines.
- **Detector (true positives):**
  - On a phone the 404 title (27.8px) barely outranks the wordmark (25.6px).
  - Aquila's bottom strip sets 14px Cormorant, clamped to two lines.
  - The rest are the previously accepted false positives.
  - CBC's favicon fails (console noise only; the fallback works).
- **Minor:**
  - "yesterday" and "24 hours ago" appear side by side.
  - Aquila's edition time has no timezone.
  - "Trending Companies" is in title case beside "US markets".
  - " - " is used where a dash is meant in about 20 strings.
