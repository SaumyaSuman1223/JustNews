# JustNews audit: 2026-09-28 (UI/UX and technical)

**Method:**
- Two independent reviewers, neither seeing the other's output:
  - A design review against the live pages and the source.
  - The Impeccable detector, run over the source and injected into the live pages.
- My own technical checks.

**Scope:**
- A production build against the local API, after a fresh ingest (1,875 new articles).
- 17 routes, in light and dark mode.
- Widths: 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320, plus 200% zoom with large text.
- Checks:
  - axe (WCAG 2.2 AA)
  - touch-target sizes
  - overflow
  - page weight
  - LCP and CLS
  - screenshots of every surface

**Not covered:**
- Signed-in flows (no test account).
- Local data has almost no multi-source story clusters, so story pages return 404 and "N sources" rarely appears.
- Market Outlook shows only Bitcoin, because there is no Finnhub key locally.

## "AI slop": what reads as templated or cloned

The front page is Perplexity Discover in warm paper. Remove the wordmark and nothing on `/en` says this is a multilingual product.

- **"Make it yours"** (`components/rail/DiscoverRail.tsx`, `i18n.ts:550`): Perplexity's heading and near-verbatim copy ("Select topics and interests to customize your Discover experience"), over a cloud of 20 pill chips.
- **The rail widgets** (`components/rail/*`):
  - Weather, Market Outlook (SPY/QQQ/DIA/VIXY/BTC in USD for every locale) and Trending Companies (US big tech).
  - None of them relate to cross-lingual news, and they are US-centric for a global product.
- **Discover's bar** (`Discover.tsx`): a "Share this feed" button and a sliders icon that repeats the rail's own Customize link.
- **Card styling:** 14px card radius and 12px image radius (`globals.css`, `.story--card`, `.story__media`). They bypass `--radius-editorial: 3px`, whose own comment warns against `rounded-2xl` on everything.
- **Letter circles instead of favicons** ("B", "T", "N") on every card, because `homepage_url` is empty (`StoryCard.tsx`). They read as placeholder avatars.
- **Four slogans on Aquila** (`i18n.ts`):
  - "The world in context"
  - "Better information builds a better tomorrow."
  - "Curated news for a more informed world"
  - "Read deeper. See further."

  "Curated" overclaims for a page laid out by software.
- **Search's subtitle** "What are you trying to understand?" is AI-assistant phrasing over a keyword search.
- **The sign-in card:** a centred card with a thin border and wide shadow (`.auth-card`), "Welcome back" for first-time visitors, and a second copy of the wordmark already in the sidebar.

## Stale: what belongs to an older product

- **Share image and app icons** (`app/[locale]/opengraph-image.tsx`, `lib/icon.tsx`): a green "JustNews" in a generic sans on cool grey. Every shared link and home-screen icon shows the old brand.
- **No favicon at all:** nothing in the `<head>`, and `/favicon.ico` returns 404.
- **The old topic page** `/desk/{id}`:
  - Understand/Latest tabs, uppercase micro-labels ("WHAT'S HAPPENING", "WHO IS SAYING WHAT"), "0 STORIES".
  - An empty-state action labelled "My Desk" that goes to a redirect.
  - The only place topic Perspectives render.
- **Pages still in the old list style,** with letter-spaced caps labels: source pages, search results, and the article page's "More from…" lists.
- **Leftover copy:** `aquila.none.action` still says "Go to Home". "Back to the front page" means Discover, while Aquila also has a "Front page".
- **How it works** explains Perspectives, which only the legacy page shows.
- **Two signed-out patterns:**
  - Saved, Following and History have a titled page with a prompt.
  - Settings, Onboarding and Feedback show a bare "Sign in to see this".
- **Display** has a body-size h1 and a Save button for a radio choice.
- **Privacy** has bold sans h2s and a public "Draft, not legal advice" banner.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] The product's reason to exist is invisible on the front page.**
   - *What:*
     - Cards carry no language.
     - No module shows the same story across languages.
     - A signed-out reader gets exactly one language, tied to the interface locale: `/hi` was 26 of 26 Hindi cards, `/en` all English.
   - *Why it matters:* PRODUCT.md names cross-lingual clustering as the wedge. As built, Discover is a monolingual aggregator.
   - *Fix:*
     - Put the language on every card's source line ("4 sources · English, हिन्दी"); the data exists.
     - Replace a US widget with "Across languages today", built from multi-language clusters.
     - Separate reading languages from the interface language with a signed-out "Read in: EN + HI" control, stored in a cookie.
     - Build and test with seeded clusters, since local ingest produces almost none.
   - *Commands:* `/impeccable shape`, then `/impeccable bolder`.
2. **[P1] On a phone, the news starts more than a screen down, and the tab bar is broken.**
   - *What:*
     - The first screen is the 20-chip interests form, then the widget row.
     - The tab bar's own `display: flex` and 68px height are overridden by the shared touch-target rule (`globals.css`, the `.tabbar__link, .locale-switcher a, …` block sets `inline-flex` and `44px`). Tabs shrink to their text, and "Discover" sits against the screen edge.
   - *Fix:*
     - Lead story and three cards first; the interests prompt inline after them, collapsed to about six chips plus "More"; widgets below the first block.
     - Take `.tabbar__link` out of the shared rule.
   - *Commands:* `/impeccable adapt`, `/impeccable onboard`.
3. **[P1] The chrome is Perplexity's, not JustNews's.**
   - *What:* see the "AI slop" list above.
   - *Fix:*
     - Rebuild the rail from things only this product has: "Today's Aquila" (the latest edition's front page as a teaser), trending, blindspots, and cross-language coverage.
     - Make markets opt-in and locale-aware (Nifty/Sensex for `hi`).
     - Rewrite the interests copy in the product's voice.
     - Drop Share-this-feed and the duplicate sliders icon.
     - Put cards on the editorial radius.
     - Cut Aquila to one motto.
   - *Commands:* `/impeccable distill`, `/impeccable clarify`.
4. **[P1] Tapping a story usually lands on a page that repeats the card.**
   - *What:*
     - `/a/{id}` shows the same title, image and snippet, then "Read the full story at…", so most stories take two taps.
     - Its hero is often a publisher thumbnail stretched to 680px (the BBC's 240px rendition), so it looks soft.
     - Imagery is untidy elsewhere too:
       - The Hindu's default crest appears as a "photo" on several cards.
       - Failed images leave empty grey boxes.
       - The first `/hi` card showed a broken-image icon.
   - *Fix:*
     - For a story with no cluster and no other languages, link straight to the publisher after logging the click. Otherwise make the page earn the click with coverage, a timeline and other languages.
     - Request larger renditions where the URL allows it.
     - On the backend, drop an image a source repeats across many articles.
     - On the client, switch to the text-only card when an image fails.
   - *Commands:* `/impeccable shape`, `/impeccable harden`.
5. **[P2] Three design languages.**
   - *What:* Discover's cards, the old list pages (source, search, read-next, `/desk`, Display, Privacy), and Aquila.
   - *Fix:*
     - Make Discover's card set, on the editorial tokens, the one system.
     - Rebuild source and search with it.
     - Retire `/desk/{id}`, or port Perspectives into Discover's topic view.
     - Unify the signed-out gates.
     - Replace the share image, icons and favicon with the current wordmark.
   - *Commands:* `/impeccable polish`, `/impeccable typeset`.

## Technical findings

**P1**
- **Buttons do not inherit the page font.** `.chip` and `.interests__chip` are `<button>`s without `font: inherit`, so they render in the browser's default font rather than Plex. With no system Devanagari font installed, the Hindi interest chips render as empty boxes. Fix: add a global `button, input, select { font: inherit }`.
- **Supabase loads on every page for one button.** `components/AccountMenu.tsx` imports the browser client for its "Sign out" button, so about 245 KB raw (64 KB gzipped) of Supabase's code is on every page. Fix: import it on click, or sign out through a server route.
- **Images are oversized.** 18 of the 26 images on Discover are downloaded at more than twice their display size: 896 KB on desktop and 1.4 MB on search. This still needs the image-service decision from the last audit.
- **The phone tab bar cascade bug** (priority issue 2).

**P2**
- **Target size (WCAG 2.5.8)** fails on every page:
  - The sidebar's "How it works" and "Send feedback" links are 15px tall.
  - Aquila's "Page 2"-style references are 11–17px tall.

  On touch screens, card Save and "…" buttons are 32px, the menu button is 36px, and search filters are 27px tall.
- **Mixed-language headlines have no `lang`.** Discover cards set no `lang` on titles. In a feed that mixes Hindi and English, screen readers use the wrong voice, and the `:lang(hi)` leading fix never applies.
- **The 404 page** has no `<title>`, no app shell and no search.
- **The micro type step (0.68rem, 10.88px)** sizes functional text: phone tab labels, Aquila page references, the search "Go straight to" label, "Filed under". 0.7rem would clear the detector's 11px floor.
- **Headlines are line-clamped** ("…ends th…"), and cards other than the lead show no time.
- **Aquila scales the page by a fractional factor** (`transform: scale()`). In headless Chromium, small text loses word spaces ("lackof fairnesscanmake"). This needs checking in real Chrome and Safari; if it reproduces, round the scale or use `zoom`.

**P3**
- The global reduced-motion rule sets 0.01ms everywhere, which also removes useful feedback transitions.
- Aquila shows two identical ≡ icons, top left and top right.
- The disabled "Save interests" button is a near-invisible grey slab.
- Search shows both "Go straight to NDTV India" and a "Sources: NDTV India" block.
- `/admin` has no h1.

**Detector:**
- The source scan is clean: 0 findings over 109 files, confirmed with canaries.
- In the live pages, the true positives are the 10.88px micro text and the auth card's border-and-shadow.
- The false positives, each checked: tight leading on display headlines, the cream ground (a documented brand choice), all-caps on Aquila's colophon, and the phone widget scroller being read as overflow.
- Injection needed CSP bypassed, because the site's production CSP blocks outside scripts, as intended.

## What's working

- **Aquila Tribune:**
  - The edition and volume line, section index, halftone, "Page 3" teasers, contents drawer and page turn.
  - It's the one surface that could only belong to this product, and the peak of the experience.
- **The foundation:**
  - Cormorant with Plex on warm paper.
  - A dark mode designed, not inverted.
  - Visible brass focus rings.
  - Devanagari leading that holds on `/hi`.
- **Honest, sturdy mechanics:**
  - The first page is rendered on the server.
  - Tabs have shareable URLs.
  - "Not interested" can be undone.
  - Market moves show an arrow and a sign, not just colour.
  - The consent banner gives Decline and Accept equal weight.
  - axe is clean apart from target size.
  - No overflow from 320px up, and no layout shift.

  - For You and Top show the same first stories.
  - "Aquila" is an unexplained name.
  - The heart jumps to the login page and loses their place.
  - Login says "Welcome back".
- **Casey (one-handed phone):**
  - The first screen is a form.
  - Tabs are lopsided, with narrow targets.
  - Two taps per story.
  - Card actions are 32px.
- **Sam (keyboard and screen reader):**
  - 13 tab stops before the first story; the skip link helps.
  - About 4 stops per card × 30 cards, with no section headings to jump between.
  - The card "…" menu has no arrow-key support.
- **Priya (bilingual Hindi and English, Delhi):**
  - She cannot read both languages together without signing in.
  - No card shows its language or its coverage elsewhere.
  - Markets are US funds in dollars.
  - Hindi interest chips fall back to a system font, or to empty boxes.

## Questions

- Remove the wordmark from `/en`: which single module proves this isn't Perplexity?
- Who is Market Outlook for, and why does a Delhi reader get VIXY in dollars?
- Aquila is the most authored thing here. Why is it a side door rather than the front page?
- Does `/a/{id}` earn its click for a story with one source and one language?
- Is the language switcher an interface setting or a reading setting, and what should Priya press to read both?
